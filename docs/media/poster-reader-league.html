4 · The verdict

The NIE is the ID card for software the official registry doesn't know yet — issued only when human and notary agree, and always signed: who, when, and on what evidence.

At first it asks often; with every answer, less. The falling curve is the system earning intuition — while intuition never signs anything: the notary always does.
